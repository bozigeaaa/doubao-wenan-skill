# Ahmed bin Saeed witnesses signing of Dubai Holding’s landmark AED5 billion construction contract

9/9/2026

Dubai, United Arab Emirates, 09 September 2026: His Highness Sheikh Ahmed bin Saeed Al Maktoum, Chairman of Dubai Holding, witnessed the signing of an AED5 billion construction contract between Dubai Holding and China State Construction Engineering Corporation Middle East (CSCEC ME) for the delivery of the Group’s new headquarters and Jumeirah Residences Emirates Towers.  

The building contract represents the single largest Dubai Holding has awarded to date, reflecting the scale of capital the Group continues to deploy into projects that support Dubai's long-term growth.

Set to become one of Dubai's most distinctive corporate landmarks, the new headquarters will bring the Group's businesses together in a workplace built around collaboration, sustainability and employee wellbeing, while opening a prime new address in one of the city's most sought-after commercial locations. The building is scheduled to open in 2029. The award will also advance the delivery of Jumeirah Residences Emirates Towers, comprising 754 branded residences across two towers in the same location, adding a striking new presence to the city’s luxury residential market.

His Excellency Mohamed bin Hadi Al Hussaini, Minister of State for Financial Affairs; and Her Excellency Ou Boqian, Consul General of the People's Republic of China in Dubai and the Northern Emirates, also attended the signing ceremony alongside Amit Kaushal, Group Chief Executive Officer of Dubai Holding; Tian Sanchuan, Chairman of CSCEC ME and senior representatives from both organisations.

His Highness Sheikh Ahmed bin Saeed Al Maktoum, Chairman of Dubai Holding, said: “Dubai’s success has been built on a clear vision for the future, the confidence to invest with purpose and the discipline to turn ambition into progress. We continue to build from a position of strength, committing capital to assets that support economic growth and create value for our people, communities and future generations.

# TDP Wins 132kV High-Voltage Cable Contract for Expo 2030 Riyadh Power Infrastructure

August 28, 2026 Mohammad S Power

Image: 1787758419521

Trading and Development Partnership (TDP) has been selected as an execution partner for the power supply infrastructure project for Expo 2030 Riyadh, in collaboration with Saudi Energy and Expo 2030 Riyadh Company.

Under the awarded contract, TDP will undertake the supply, installation, testing and commissioning of 132kV high-voltage cables to connect the main electrical substations serving the Expo 2030 Riyadh site directly to the national electricity grid.

The project will provide a reliable and sustainable power supply for the Expo site, which is planned to cover approximately 6 million square metres, supporting the large-scale infrastructure and operational requirements of the global event.

The scope of work includes the engineering and execution of 132kV cable systems, including cable supply, installation, testing and commissioning, as part of the electrical infrastructure required to establish reliable connections between the Expo’s main substations and the national grid.

The project is being developed not only to meet the power requirements of Expo 2030 Riyadh but also to establish future-ready electrical infrastructure capable of supporting Riyadh’s long-term urban development. The infrastructure is expected to form part of the city’s wider sustainable and resilient utility network beyond the duration of the Expo.

The contract signing ceremony was attended by senior executives from the participating organizations. Sami Al Zahrani, Group CEO of TDP, and Mohamed Fadel, CEO of TDP Power & Service, led the company’s delegation. The agreement was signed with Waleed Al Saadi, CEO of National Grid, in the presence of Khalid bin Salem Al Ghamdi, CEO of Saudi Energy, Talal Al Marri, CEO of Expo 2030 Riyadh, and Abdulaziz Alaqel, CEO of Development at Expo 2030 Riyadh.

The award represents a significant milestone for TDP and strengthens its role in delivering critical high-voltage power infrastructure for major projects in the Kingdom. The company will leverage its engineering expertise and execution capabilities to support one of Saudi Arabia’s most ambitious international developments.

Expo 2030 Riyadh is a major component of the Kingdom’s transformation agenda and is expected to attract visitors from around the world. The development of reliable electricity infrastructure is therefore a critical element in enabling the Expo’s facilities and supporting Riyadh’s continued growth as a global city.

The project also aligns with the objectives of Saudi Vision 2030, particularly the development of sustainable infrastructure, strengthening local execution capabilities, and positioning Riyadh as a leading global destination for business, innovation and international events.

With the delivery of the 132kV power infrastructure, TDP will contribute to powering Expo 2030 Riyadh while helping establish electrical infrastructure designed to support the future development of the Saudi capital.--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Saudi Arabia’s 1.8 GW Taiba 2 CCGT Project Achieves First Fire Milestone

August 29, 2026 Mohammad S Power

Image: 1787579614002

The 1.8 GW Taiba 2 Combined-Cycle Gas Turbine (CCGT) Power Project in Saudi Arabia has achieved a major commissioning milestone with the successful first fire of Gas Turbine GT11, marking a significant step toward the project’s commercial operation.

The Taiba 2 Independent Power Project (IPP) was awarded by the Saudi Power Procurement Company (SPPC) to a consortium comprising EDF, Al-Jomaih Energy and Water and Buhur for Investment. The project forms part of Saudi Arabia’s major IPP program aimed at expanding the Kingdom’s power generation capacity to meet growing electricity demand.(read more)

The first-fire milestone has been achieved by the Taiba 2 project team under the technical leadership of EDF, as part of the project’s commissioning and testing program.

The project is being developed with the support of key Saudi stakeholders, including SPPC as the Principal Buyer, the Ministry of Energy (MoE), the Saudi Energy Regulatory Authority (SERA), and the Local Content and Government Procurement Authority (LCGPA).

Saudi Energy, formerly known as Saudi Electricity Company (SEC), has signed an agreement with Expo 2030 Riyadh Company to develop a dedicated electricity supply infrastructure for the Expo 2030 Riyadh site, strengthening the power network supporting one of the Kingdom’s largest upcoming international events.

# Saudi Arabia’s 1.8GW Taiba 2 plant reaches first-fire milestone

By Abdullah Naneesh | 30 August 2026

Image: Saudi Arabia’s 1.8GW Taiba 2 plant reaches first-fire milestone

Saudi Arabia’s 1.8GW Taiba 2 combined-cycle gas-fired power plant has achieved first fire on its initial gas-turbine unit, moving the project into a critical stage of testing and commissioning ahead of commercial operation.
The milestone concerns turbine GT11 and follows the completion of the construction and pre-commissioning work needed to introduce fuel and operate the machine for the first time. First fire allows project teams to begin a wider sequence of turbine testing, synchronisation and performance-verification activities.
Taiba 2 is being developed as an independent power project under a consortium comprising EDF, Aljomaih Energy & Water and Buhur for Investment. The Saudi Power Procurement Company is the principal buyer, with the project forming part of the kingdom’s programme to add efficient generating capacity to the electricity system.
