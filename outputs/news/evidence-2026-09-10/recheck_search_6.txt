August 29, 2026 Mohammad S Power

Image: 1787579614002

The 1.8 GW Taiba 2 Combined-Cycle Gas Turbine (CCGT) Power Project in Saudi Arabia has achieved a major commissioning milestone with the successful first fire of Gas Turbine GT11, marking a significant step toward the project’s commercial operation.

The Taiba 2 Independent Power Project (IPP) was awarded by the Saudi Power Procurement Company (SPPC) to a consortium comprising EDF, Al-Jomaih Energy and Water and Buhur for Investment. The project is being developed with the support of key Saudi stakeholders, including SPPC as the Principal Buyer, the Ministry of Energy (MoE), the Saudi Energy Regulatory Authority (SERA), and the Local Content and Government Procurement Authority (LCGPA).

# Saudi Arabia’s 1.8GW Taiba 2 plant reaches first-fire milestone

By Abdullah Naneesh | 30 August 2026

Image: Saudi Arabia’s 1.8GW Taiba 2 plant reaches first-fire milestone

Saudi Arabia’s 1.8GW Taiba 2 combined-cycle gas-fired power plant has achieved first fire on its initial gas-turbine unit, moving the project into a critical stage of testing and commissioning ahead of commercial operation.
The milestone concerns turbine GT11 and follows the completion of the construction and pre-commissioning work needed to introduce fuel and operate the machine for the first time. First fire allows project teams to begin a wider sequence of turbine testing, synchronisation and performance-verification activities.
Taiba 2 is being developed as an independent power project under a consortium comprising EDF, Aljomaih Energy & Water and Buhur for Investment. The Saudi Power Procurement Company is the principal buyer, with the project forming part of the kingdom’s programme to add efficient generating capacity to the electricity system.

# Saudi Arabia’s Taiba-2 CCGT Plant Achieves First Fire on 1.8GW Gas Turbine

The Taiba-2 combined-cycle power plant under construction in Saudi Arabia announced on August 29, 2026 that it has achieved first fire on Gas Turbine Unit 11 (GT11), part of the plant’s 1.8GW capacity. The project was awarded by the Saudi Power Procurement Company (SPPC), with development and construction led by a consortium of France’s EDF, Al-Jomaih Energy and Water, and Buhur for Investment, according to the report.

The Taiba-2 project is a gas turbine-based combined-cycle (CCGT, Combined Cycle Gas Turbine) power plant designed with provisions for future carbon capture equipment, it is reported. The first fire milestone marks the entry of Gas Turbine Unit 11 into the commissioning phase, described as one of the final major steps before commercial operation. EDF is reported to be leading the plant’s commissioning, while the specific gas turbine manufacturer and other equipment suppliers have not been disclosed.

The Taiba-2 combined-cycle power plant has a generating capacity of 1.8GW, and Gas Turbine Unit 11 (GT11) achieved first fire on August 29, 2026, according to the report. The client is the Saudi Power Procurement Company (SPPC), and the development consortium comprises EDF, Al-Jomaih Energy and Water, and Buhur for Investment.

The Taiba-2 project is reported to be part of Saudi Arabia’s Independent Power Producer (IPP) program aimed at expanding gas turbine-based generation capacity to meet rising electricity demand. The carbon-capture-ready design is said to be aimed at preparing for tighter future emissions regulations.

The sequential progress of large gas turbine power projects to the first fire stage illustrates both the Middle East’s growing power infrastructure demand and the project management capabilities of international power development consortia. Carbon-capture-ready design could become a more standard design criterion for future power projects.
