#### AGGIUDICATO AL CONSULENTE IL PROJECT MANAGEMENT DEL PROGETTO FERROVIARIO per il fosfato di SAR

La società di ingegneria statunitense Aecom si è aggiudicata un contratto da parte di Saudi Arabian Railways (SAR) per la fornitura di servizi di consulenza e gestione progetto (project management consultancy) relativi al programma ferroviario Phosphate 3. L'accordo, della durata di 54 mesi, riguarda il potenziamento e il raddoppio dei binari lungo la rete di trasporto dei fosfati che collega le miniere di Waad Al-Shamal al porto industriale di Ras Al-Khair, con un primo lotto di circa 100 chilometri tra l'area di AZ1/Nariyah e la costa. L'esecuzione dei lavori di costruzione di questo lotto, per un valore stimato di oltre 4 miliardi di riyal (circa 1,1 miliardi di dollari), è stata affidata alla ditta locale Alomaier Trading & Contracting Company, affiancata dalla svizzera ARX in qualità di consulente tecnico. L'iniziativa costituisce il primo di quattro pacchetti previsti per potenziare la linea merci del Nord (storicamente nota come North-South Railway). L'aggiunta del secondo binario e l'ampliamento degli scali merci aumenteranno significativamente la capacità di carico e la logistica mineraria verso i complessi di produzione di fertilizzanti di Ras Al-Khair. Parallelamente, la compagnia statale SAR sta valutando la localizzazione della catena di fornitura per i materiali ferroviari, inclusa la realizzazione di uno stabilimento locale per la produzione di traversine in cemento.       Fonte notizia:Meed https://www.meed.com/consultant-wins-sar-phosphate-rail-project-management (ICE RIYADH)

# Saudi Arabia Launches RFQ for Suburb Boulevard PPP Project

Date August 31, 2026

Contracting Authorities Ministry of Municipalities and Housing (MOMAH)

Type Project in tender

Subscribe to our newsletter and get the latest news and business opportunities in your inbox

Image: Saudi Arabia Launches RFQ for Suburb Boulevard PPP Project

The Ministry of Municipalities and Housing (MOMAH), in partnership with the National Center for Privatization & PPP (NCP) and Ashraq Development Company, the development arm of the Eastern Province Municipality, has launched the Request for Qualification (RFQ) phase for the King Fahd Suburb Boulevard Project in the Eastern Province.

The project is planned as a Public-Private Partnership (PPP) under a Design, Build, Finance, Operate, Maintain and Transfer (DBFOMT) model, with a total concession period of 43 years. Situated in Al Bayda Governorate in Saudi Arabia’s Eastern Province, the King Fahd Suburb Boulevard Project will span approximately 1 million square metres and will be implemented in two phases. Under the proposed PPP arrangement, the appointed private-sector partner will oversee the project’s design, development, financing, construction, operation and maintenance, creating an integrated mixed-use urban destination. At the conclusion of the concession term, the project will be transferred back to the public sector. 

The scope of work includes the development of a 4-km integrated mixed-use zone extending along a central boulevard and linear parklands, forming part of a wider 7.3-km corridor. The master development will feature a balanced mix of development plots, green spaces and supporting infrastructure. The project will also include a range of leisure and recreational facilities, public parks, entertainment venues, retail outlets, office spaces and road networks, creating a vibrant urban environment and enhancing the quality of life for residents and visitors in the Eastern Province.

The King Fahd Suburb Boulevard Project is expected to contribute significantly to the transformation of the urban environment in Dammam’s King Fahd Suburb, while opening up substantial opportunities for private-sector investment and participation in Saudi Arabia’s municipal infrastructure and urban development sectors.

## Saudi Arabia launches RFQs for King Fahd Suburb Boulevard project

Image: Saudi Arabia launches RFQs for King Fahd Suburb Boulevard project

Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Municipalities and Housing (MOMAH), in partnership with the National Center for Privatization & PPP (NCP) and Ashraq Development Company, the development arm of the Eastern Province Municipality, has launched the Request for Qualification (RFQ) phase for the King Fahd Suburb Boulevard Project in the Eastern Province.

The project will be developed through a Public-Private Partnership (PPP) under a Design, Build, Finance, Operate, Maintain and Transfer (DBFOMT) model, with a total contract term of 43 years.

Located in Al Bayda Governorate in the Eastern Province, the King Fahd Suburb Boulevard Project covers an area of approximately 1 million square metres and will be delivered in two development phases.

Under the proposed PPP agreement, the selected private-sector partner will be responsible for the design, development, financing, construction, operation and maintenance of a comprehensive mixed-use urban destination, before transferring the project back at the end of the concession period.

The scope of work includes the development of a 4-kilometre integrated mixed-use zone extending along a central boulevard and linear parklands, forming part of a wider 7.3-kilometre corridor. The master development will feature a balanced mix of development plots, green spaces and supporting infrastructure.

The project will also include a range of leisure and recreational facilities, public parks, entertainment venues, retail outlets, office spaces and road networks, creating a vibrant urban environment and enhancing the quality of life for residents and visitors in the Eastern Province.

The King Fahd Suburb Boulevard Project is expected to play a key role in reshaping the urban landscape of the King Fahd Suburb area in Dammam while creating significant opportunities for private-sector participation in the Kingdom’s municipal infrastructure and urban development sector.

The project supports the objectives of Saudi Vision 2030, particularly initiatives aimed at improving quality of life, promoting sustainable urban development and increasing private-sector investment and participation in infrastructure projects. -TradeArabia News Service
