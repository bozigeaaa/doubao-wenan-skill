Riyadh Cement Awards $14.9 Million Digital Transformation Contract to Tianjin Cement Institute
==============================================================================================

[September 14, 2026](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/2026/09/)
[Mohammad S](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/author/ksa-projects/)
[Manufacturing](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/category/manufacturing/)

![](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/cement-678x381.jpg "cement")

Riyadh Cement Company has awarded a **$14.9 million contract, excluding VAT, to Tianjin Cement Industry Design & Research Institute Co., Ltd.** for the implementation of its **Light House Project**, aimed at accelerating the company’s digital transformation and improving the efficiency and sustainability of its operations.

The contract was signed on **September 14, 2026**, with a duration of **24 months**.

Under the agreement, Tianjin Cement Industry Design & Research Institute will implement the Light House Project in accordance with the agreed scope of work, focusing on the deployment of **digital technologies, automation, Industry 4.0 applications, artificial intelligence, advanced data analytics, and process optimization**.

Advertisement

The project will also include the implementation of **predictive maintenance and energy-efficiency solutions**, together with other related activities designed to improve operational performance across Riyadh Cement’s operations.

The initiative is expected to enhance **operational efficiency and productivity, reduce operating costs, improve asset performance, and strengthen sustainability** through the increased use of digital technologies and data-driven decision-making.

Riyadh Cement said the project forms part of its broader **operational and digital transformation strategy**, which is focused on strengthening the company’s competitiveness and supporting its long-term growth objectives.

The financial impact of the project will be reflected in Riyadh Cement’s financial results according to the implementation phases of the contract, with the operational and financial benefits expected to be realized progressively throughout the project period.

To enable the full implementation and realization of the Light House Project, Riyadh Cement will also undertake supporting investments in **infrastructure development, value-added buildings, and related equipment**, as required.

The project represents a significant step in Riyadh Cement’s efforts to modernize its production and operational processes and leverage **Industry 4.0 and digital transformation technologies** to build more efficient, productive and sustainable cement operations.
