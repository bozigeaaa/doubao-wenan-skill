Saudi Arabia Identifies Estimated 110 Million Tonnes of Ore at Jabal Sayid Rare Earth Project
=============================================================================================

[September 14, 2026](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/2026/09/)
[Mohammad S](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/author/ksa-projects/)
[Mining](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/category/mining/)

![](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Mining1-678x381.jpg "Mining1")

Saudi Arabia has announced the discovery of an estimated **110 million tons of mineral ore** containing high concentrations of **rare earth elements (REEs)**, particularly heavy rare earth elements, along with promising concentrations of **uranium**, at the **Jabal Sayid project in Madinah Province**.

The discovery was announced by **Energy Minister Prince Abdulaziz bin Salman** during the **International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) General Conference in Vienna**. According to the minister, the scale and composition of the identified resources could place Jabal Sayid among the world’s significant rare earth mineral resource sites currently under development.

The geological exploration program at Jabal Sayid has identified substantial mineralized resources with potential for the recovery of critical minerals, including heavy rare earth elements, which are essential raw materials for a range of advanced technologies and strategic industries.

Advertisement

In addition to the rare earth resources, cooperation between Saudi Arabia and French nuclear and energy company **Orano** has identified **economic potential for recovering uranium** from the project’s operations.

The potential uranium recovery forms part of Saudi Arabia’s broader strategy to develop its domestic uranium resources and strengthen the Kingdom’s capabilities across the nuclear fuel cycle. The Kingdom is pursuing a national program to explore uranium resources and maximize their economic value through several interconnected initiatives.

One of the initiatives involves the **recovery of uranium as a byproduct of phosphoric acid production** associated with phosphate fertilizer manufacturing in northern Saudi Arabia. Saudi Arabia is one of the world’s leading phosphate fertilizer exporters.

The Jabal Sayid discovery also supports Saudi Arabia’s wider efforts to develop a more diversified and sustainable national energy mix, including the development of **nuclear energy capabilities through international partnerships**.
