Jizan, September 14, 2026, SPA -- Construction of the ready-built industrial units project in Jazan City for Primary and Downstream Industries is progressing, with completion reaching 52%, to provide an integrated industrial environment that enables investors to launch operations within modern infrastructure, while offering support services designed to meet their operational needs.

Covering a total area of 154,490 square meters, the project comprises 38 ready-built industrial units of varying sizes. The units are designed to provide investors with flexible options suited to the nature and scale of their industrial activities.

Upon completion, the project is expected to boost local production and support industrial growth. It will also improve the investment climate by providing enterprises with the infrastructure and support needed to expand and sustain their operations.
