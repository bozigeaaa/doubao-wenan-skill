Arabian Drilling Wins SAR 2 Billion Five-Year Contract from SLB for 8 Gas Land Rigs
===================================================================================

[September 14, 2026](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/2026/09/)
[Mohammad S](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/author/ksa-projects/)
[Oil and Gas](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/category/oil-and-gas/)

![](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Drilling-678x381.jpg "Drilling")

Arabian Drilling Company, Saudi Arabia’s largest drilling contractor by fleet size, has secured a new **five-year contract from SLB** for the provision of **eight additional land rigs for Gas LSTK operations in the Kingdom**.

The contract represents an incremental **backlog of approximately SAR 2 billion** and is expected to contribute to Arabian Drilling’s revenues beginning in the **fourth quarter of 2026**.

Under the contract, Arabian Drilling will provide **eight land drilling rigs** to support SLB’s Gas LSTK operations. The rigs will be redeployed from Arabian Drilling’s existing available land rig fleet, expanding the company’s active onshore operations and increasing utilization of its land rig assets.

Advertisement

The latest award follows Arabian Drilling’s previously announced **five-year contract with SLB for 11 existing land rigs** for Gas LSTK operations. Together, the two contracts cover a total of **19 land rigs** and add approximately **SAR 5 billion to Arabian Drilling’s backlog**.

Following the awards, the company’s total backlog is expected to reach approximately **SAR 16 billion**, representing the highest backlog in Arabian Drilling’s history.

The two contracts also represent the company’s **largest-ever Gas LSTK commitment**, reinforcing Arabian Drilling’s position in Saudi Arabia’s domestic gas drilling market. The company will maintain a **100% share of domestic Gas LSTK operations** under the current awards.

Arabian Drilling said the contracts will strengthen its long-term revenue visibility and support higher land fleet utilization while enabling the company to contribute to the Kingdom’s expanding gas development program.

Commenting on the award, **Fahad Albani, Chief Executive Officer of Arabian Drilling**, said the eight-rig contract reflects the strength of the company’s operating model and the continued confidence of its client in Arabian Drilling’s capabilities.

He added that the two contracts covering 19 rigs mark a historic milestone for the company and have driven its backlog to an all-time high, while Arabian Drilling remains focused on safe and efficient execution and supporting Saudi Arabia’s growing gas program.
