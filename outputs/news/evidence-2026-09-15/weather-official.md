Riyadh, September 15, 2026, SPA -- According to the National Center for Meteorology, moderate to heavy thunderstorms, leading to flash floods and accompanied by hail, are expected in parts of Jazan, Aseer, Al-Baha, Makkah, and Madinah regions, with a chance of thunderclouds forming over parts of Najran Region. Gusty, dust-stirring winds are also expected in these regions, as well as in Eastern, Tabuk and Riyadh regions, while fog may form during the night and early morning hours in coastal areas along the Red Sea and the Arabian Gulf.

Winds over the Red Sea will be northwesterly to westerly at speeds of 15-35 km/h, and southwesterly to northwesterly at speeds of 10-50 km/h. Waves may range from half a meter to over two meters; the sea will be calm to choppy.

In the Arabian Gulf, winds will be southwesterly to southeasterly at speeds of 10-25 km/h, and southeasterly to northeasterly at speeds of 10-25 km/h. Waves will range from half a meter to one meter, and the sea will be calm.
