Riyadh Cement Signs 25-Year Solar Energy Purchase Agreement with Samana Energy
==============================================================================

[September 14, 2026](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/2026/09/)
[Mohammad S](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/author/ksa-projects/)
[Power](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/category/power/)

![Solar PV Image](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/Solar-Image-12-678x381.jpg "Solar Image 12")

Riyadh Cement Company has awarded and signed a **25-year agreement with Samana Energy** for the purchase of solar-generated electricity, supporting the company’s efforts to reduce energy costs and increase the use of clean energy in line with **Saudi Arabia’s Vision 2030** sustainability objectives.

Under the agreement, Riyadh Cement will purchase energy generated by Samana Energy’s solar power generation system at a cost expected to be lower than the company’s current electricity generation cost as well as the applicable cost of electricity from Saudi Energy Company.

The agreement has an expected **average annual payment of approximately SAR 6 million, excluding VAT**, over the 25-year contract period.

Advertisement

The agreement covers the **supply and purchase of solar energy** for Riyadh Cement’s operations. The solar power generation system is expected to commence commercial operations in the **fourth quarter of 2027**, subject to obtaining the required licenses and approvals from the relevant authorities.

A key feature of the agreement is that **Riyadh Cement will not be required to finance the project or incur capital or operating expenditures** associated with the solar generation system. Samana Energy will provide the energy supply under the long-term agreement.

The project is expected to enable Riyadh Cement to benefit from renewable energy at a competitive cost while supporting the company’s efforts to improve energy efficiency and reduce its reliance on conventional power generation.

According to Riyadh Cement, the cost savings from the agreement are expected to **materialize gradually from the first year of operations** following commencement of the solar power generation system.

The actual financial impact will depend on the energy prices applicable to the electricity consumed from the project at the time of operation.

The agreement represents a long-term strategic step by Riyadh Cement to secure competitively priced renewable electricity while supporting Saudi Arabia’s transition toward cleaner energy and the sustainability objectives of Vision 2030.
