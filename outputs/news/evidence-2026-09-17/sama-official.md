SAMA Decision on Repo and Reverse Repo Rates
============================================

[![Share](/_layouts/15/SAMA.Portal/assets/images/actions/share.svg)](#)

* [![](/_layouts/15/SAMA.Portal/assets/images/email-icon.svg)](#)
* [![](/_layouts/15/SAMA.Portal/assets/images/social/facebook-colored.svg)](#)

* [![](/_layouts/15/SAMA.Portal/assets/images/social/linkedin-colored.svg)](#)

9/16/2026 9:15 PM

The Saudi Central Bank has decided to raise the Repurchase Agreement (Repo) rate by 25 basis points to 4.50 percent, and to raise the Reverse Repurchase Agreement (Reverse Repo) rate by 25 basis points to 4.00 percent. This decision is in line with SAMA's mandate of preserving monetary stability.
