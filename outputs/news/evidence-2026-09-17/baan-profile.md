### Company Profile

**Company overview**

BAAN Holding Group is a national champion in the entertainment, hospitality, and catering sectors, with operations spanning the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and the wider MENA region. The Group manages and operates a broad portfolio of family entertainment centers and hotels across the MENA region, in addition to organizing events and special occasions. It actively contributes to the growth of the tourism sector by delivering innovative, immersive entertainment and tourism experiences. Furthermore, BAAN is engaged in the development and operation of commercial ventures across hospitality and retail, aligning with the evolving aspirations of the modern Saudi market and Vision 2030.

**Company History**

The legacy of BAAN Holding Group formerly known as Al Hokair Group began in Riyadh in 1972 with the launch of Saudi Arabia first entertainment center marking a pioneering moment in the Kingdoms leisure landscape. Officially established in 1978 with a capital of SAR 724000, the company has spent nearly 50 years building an enduring reputation in the tourism, entertainment, and hospitality sectors. Over the decades, the company solidified its presence through continuous growth, including the milestone opening of its first hotel in Riyadh in 1988.BAAN Holding has undergone significant financial and administrative transformations, including a series of capital increases that culminated in its listing on the Saudi Stock Exchange in 2014 with a capital of SAR 550 million. In 2024, the company embarked on a bold strategic transformation rebranding as BAAN Holding Group, appointing a new executive leadership team, and launching its food and catering division in NEOM. These efforts reflect BAANs renewed vision for diversified, sustainable growth and its ongoing commitment to shaping the future of entertainment and hospitality in Saudi Arabia
