BAAN Holding Launches 2,600-Sq-M Family Entertainment Project with Hasbro in Jeddah
===================================================================================

[September 16, 2026](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/2026/09/)
[Mohammad S](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/author/ksa-projects/)
[Construction](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/category/construction/)

![](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/Construction-Site-678x381.jpg "Construction Site")

**BAAN Holding Group Company** has commenced implementation works for **Playocity**, a new family entertainment project being developed in partnership with global entertainment brand **Hasbro** at **Westfield Center in Jeddah**.

The approximately **2,600-square-meter entertainment project** will deliver an integrated family entertainment destination featuring modern attractions, interactive activities and immersive experiences designed for visitors across different age groups.

Playocity will incorporate a selection of **Hasbro’s internationally recognized characters and entertainment brands**, bringing the global brand’s intellectual properties into a dedicated family entertainment destination in Jeddah.

Advertisement

The partnership with Hasbro forms a key component of the project, leveraging the global brand’s extensive presence and established portfolio of entertainment characters and brands to create a differentiated visitor experience.

### Project Scope

The Playocity development will cover approximately **2,600 square meters** and will include:

* Modern family entertainment attractions
* Interactive activities and experiences
* Immersive entertainment offerings
* Attractions and experiences based on selected Hasbro characters and entertainment brands
* Facilities designed to serve families and visitors across different age groups

Located at **Westfield Center in Jeddah**, the project is strategically positioned within an emerging retail and entertainment destination in the Kingdom.

BAAN Holding said the project forms part of its strategy to expand its presence in the **family entertainment sector**, diversify its investments into high-growth segments and capitalize on opportunities arising from the continued development of Saudi Arabia’s entertainment industry.

The development also supports the objectives of **Saudi Vision 2030** to expand the Kingdom’s entertainment offering and enhance quality of life.

BAAN Holding expects **Playocity to be completed and commence commercial operations during Q1 2027**. The company expects the project to contribute to future revenue growth, diversify its income streams and expand its customer base following the start of commercial operations.

The financial impact of the project is expected to begin reflecting in BAAN Holding’s financial results upon the commencement of commercial operations.
