# Shipping from the Mediterranean Sea to the Middle East? Here's an update for our SE3 service

GeneralStrait of HormuzEuropeMiddle East and Indian Subcontinent

![SE3_Suez2.png](https://www.hapag-lloyd.com/zh/services-information/news/2026/09/shipping-with-our-se3-service--here-s-a-rotation-update/_jcr_content/heroimage.image.img.transform/newsimage-desktop/c-1788937480640.png/a-1788937439000.png/SE3_Suez2.png)

If you are shipping with our **[SE3 service](https://www.hapag-lloyd.com/zh/services-information/routes-trades/gemini-cogh/route-finder.html/mediterranean/asia/SE3)**, here's an update to support your cargo planning.

From **September 26, 2026,** our service will operate with an updated eastbound rotation, adding **Salalah, Oman** as a port of call.

For your reference, here's an overview of the **updated SE3 rotation** including the first sailing:

- Qingdao, CN -> Kwangyang, KR -> Ningbo, CN -> Tanjung Pelepas, MY -> Jeddah, SA -> Port Said, EG -> Damietta, EG -> **Salalah, OM**-\> Colombo, LK -> Singapore, SN -> Qingdao, CN

| Vessel / Voyage | Estimated Time of Arrival | Port |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Morten Maersk V. 638E | September 26, 2026 | Salalah, OM |

To assist your cargo planning, you can use our [Schedule](https://www.hapag-lloyd.com/zh/online-business/schedule/interactive-schedule/interactive-schedule-solution.html) to view your vessel’s latest Estimated Times of Arrival and Departure. Should you have any questions or require further information, please contact our [customer service team at your location](https://www.hapag-lloyd.com/zh/services-information/offices-localinfo.html), who will guide you based on your individual situation.
