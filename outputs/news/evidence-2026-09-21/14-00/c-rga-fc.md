RGA Highlights Multiple Routes Supporting Khaleeji 27 Fans Traveling to Jeddah

Jeddah, September 20, 2026, SPA -- With Jeddah hosting the 27th Arabian Gulf Cup (Khaleeji 27), Saudi Arabia's extensive road network is helping facilitate the arrival of fans and visitors traveling to the city by land.

Spanning more than 73,000 kilometers, the Kingdom's road network connects Saudi Arabia with neighboring countries and provides access through several land border crossings. The Roads General Authority (RGA) said the network offers multiple routes for visitors arriving from Gulf states and neighboring countries, passing through several regions and governorates before reaching Jeddah.

The authority highlighted the road sector's role in supporting major sporting events and facilitating visitor movement. The integrated network also supports travel between Saudi regions and border crossings, contributing to the tourism, sports, and logistics sectors and enhancing the experience of road users.

The RGA continues to regulate and oversee the road sector and improve quality and safety standards. These efforts strengthen connectivity among cities, governorates, and centers and facilitate the movement of people and goods across the Kingdom.

\-\- SPA
