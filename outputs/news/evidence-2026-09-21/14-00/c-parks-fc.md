Arar, September 20, 2026, SPA -- The Northern Borders Municipality and its affiliated municipalities have completed preparations at more than 130 parks and recreational areas across the region's cities and governorates ahead of Saudi National Day celebrations. The preparations aim to ensure the readiness of public facilities and improve services for residents and visitors.

Northern Borders Municipality Spokesperson Mohammed bin Sabti said the preparations included readying parks, recreational areas, and public facilities, as well as carrying out cleaning and maintenance work to provide a suitable environment for visitors.

He added that the municipality and its affiliated municipalities are organizing events and programs across the region's cities, governorates, and centers to mark the occasion. Targeting different age groups, the activities include national, recreational, cultural, and social programs.

Bin Sabti said field teams will continue to monitor service levels, cleanliness, maintenance, and facility readiness throughout the holiday period to improve the visitor experience and enhance quality of life.
