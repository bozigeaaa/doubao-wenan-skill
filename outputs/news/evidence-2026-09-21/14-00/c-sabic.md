Technip Energies and SABIC Sign Exclusive Agreement for Global Licensing of CTR® LDPE Technology
================================================================================================

[September 21, 2026](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/2026/09/)
[Mohammad S](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/author/ksa-projects/)
[Petrochemical](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/category/petrochemical/)

![](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/1789737942256-678x381.jpg "1789737942256")

**Technip Energies and SABIC have entered into an exclusive Licensing Collaboration Agreement (LCA) that will enable Technip Energies to act as the worldwide licensor of SABIC’s Clean Tubular Reactor (CTR®) technology for Low-Density Polyethylene (LDPE) production.**

The agreement strengthens the long-standing collaboration between the two companies and combines **SABIC’s established CTR® technology with Technip Energies’ expertise in polyolefin technology, engineering and project execution**.

Under the agreement, Technip Energies will provide global licensing support for SABIC’s CTR® technology, enabling producers worldwide to adopt the technology for the development of high-pressure LDPE plants.

Advertisement

The collaboration covers the worldwide licensing of SABIC’s **CTR® LDPE technology**, supported by Technip Energies’ expertise in the design, fabrication of equipment and execution of high-pressure LDPE facilities.

Technip Energies has been supporting SABIC’s CTR® technology since 1996, contributing to **basic design, engineering, construction and plant start-up activities**. This long-standing involvement has provided the company with extensive operational and project execution experience related to the technology.

Stephane Mespoulhes, Senior Vice President, Ethylene and Polyolefins at Technip Energies, said the agreement represents a significant step in the companies’ long-standing partnership and strengthens Technip Energies’ position in LDPE technology licensing.

According to Technip Energies, the combination of SABIC’s proven CTR® technology and its own expertise in engineering, equipment fabrication and execution of high-pressure LDPE plants is intended to support the deployment of LDPE production solutions globally.

The agreement is expected to further expand the global deployment of CTR® technology while leveraging the combined technical and execution capabilities of SABIC and Technip Energies.
