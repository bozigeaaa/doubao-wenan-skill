HUMAIN Signs More Than SAR 8.77 Billion AI Data Center Agreement with MIS for 250MW Expansion
=============================================================================================

[September 20, 2026](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/2026/09/)
[Mohammad S](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/author/ksa-projects/)
[AI and Data Center](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/category/ai-and-data-center/)

![](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/data-center-678x381.jpg "data-center")

Future of Artificial Intelligence Company (**HUMAIN**) has formally signed an agreement with **Al Moammar Information Systems Company (MIS)** for the design and construction of AI-focused data centers in Saudi Arabia, with the total agreement value exceeding **689% of MIS’s FY2025 revenue, including VAT**.

Based on MIS’s reported FY2025 revenue, the disclosed percentage represents an indicative value of **more than SAR 8.77 billion**. However, MIS has not disclosed a specific fixed contract value in Saudi Riyals.

The agreement, signed on **September 19, 2026**, expands the planned capacity of the AI-focused data center project from the previously announced **50 MW to 250 MW**, representing an additional **200 MW** of planned capacity.

Advertisement

### Project Scope

Under the agreement, MIS will undertake the **engineering, procurement and construction (EPC)** works for AI-focused data center facilities for HUMAIN.

The expanded project will increase the overall planned capacity from 50 MW to **250 MW**, with the additional capacity expected to be delivered through work orders issued by HUMAIN in accordance with the terms and conditions of the agreement.

The facilities are intended to provide large-scale digital infrastructure dedicated to artificial intelligence technologies, supporting the growing demand for advanced computing and AI capabilities in Saudi Arabia.

### Work Orders to Determine Project Execution

MIS said that the EPC works will be executed through individual **work orders issued by HUMAIN**. The work orders are expected to be received sequentially, with MIS to announce the value, execution period and expected financial impact of each material work order upon receipt.

The agreement itself does not specify a fixed contract duration. The financial impact on MIS will be reflected as the relevant work orders are received and executed.

### Agreement Supersedes Earlier Contract

MIS also confirmed that the new agreement **supersedes the contract previously announced on Tadawul on March 5, 2026**, relating to the AI-focused data center project.

According to the company, the new agreement does not affect the value, progress or financial impact previously disclosed in connection with the earlier contract. The financial impact from the previously announced works had been expected to commence from the **second quarter of FY2026**.

The latest agreement formalizes the expanded scope and increases the project’s planned capacity fivefold, from **50 MW to 250 MW**.

### Project Snapshot

* **Project:** AI-Focused Data Centers
* **Client:** HUMAIN
* **Contractor:** Al Moammar Information Systems Company (MIS)
* **Location:** Saudi Arabia
* **Contract Value:** More than 689% of MIS FY2025 revenue, including VAT; equivalent to more than **SAR 8.77 billion** based on MIS’s FY2025 revenue
* **Original Planned Capacity:** 50 MW
* **Expanded Planned Capacity:** 250 MW
* **Additional Capacity:** 200 MW
* **Scope:** Engineering, Procurement and Construction (EPC) of AI-focused data centers
* **Execution:** Through sequential work orders issued by HUMAIN
* **Agreement Signed:** September 19, 2026

The agreement further expands the development of large-scale AI and data center infrastructure in Saudi Arabia, with MIS responsible for delivering the engineering and construction works for the increased 250 MW planned capacity.
