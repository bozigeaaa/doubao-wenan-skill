India’s Megha Engineering Secures US$379 Million KOC Contract for GC-25 and GC-30 Facilities
============================================================================================

[September 19, 2026](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/2026/09/)
[Mohammad S](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/author/ksa-projects/)
[Oil and Gas](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/category/oil-and-gas/)

![](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/oil-and-gas-scaled-e1743587129242-678x381.jpeg)

[**Kuwait Oil Company (KOC)**](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/tag/kuwait-oil-company/) has awarded **Megha Engineering & Infrastructures Limited (MEIL)** a **KWD 117 million (approximately US$381 million)** contract for the development of a **Separation Facility at Gathering Centre 25 (GC-25) and a Water Injection Facility at Gathering Centre 30 (GC-30)** in Kuwait.

The contract follows MEIL’s submission of the lowest bid of **KWD 117 million** for the [project during the tender process in November 2025.](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/2025/11/indias-megha-engineering-submits-lowest-bids-of-995-6-million-for-koc-projects/) The award was subsequently approved by Kuwait’s Supreme Purchasing Committee for Kuwait Petroleum Corporation.

The project forms part of KOC’s upstream infrastructure development program and involves the construction of new processing and water-treatment facilities at GC-25 and GC-30, together with connecting pipelines, tie-ins, utilities and associated infrastructure.

Advertisement

### Project Scope

At **GC-25**, MEIL’s scope includes the development of a new three-phase low-pressure wet separation facility, including **two 150 MBLPD wet separator packages**, an **LP gas knock-out drum with a capacity of 53 MMSCFD**, and associated gas handling facilities.

The facility will also include an effluent water treatment and transfer system, comprising an **effluent water balance tank with micro-bubble flotation/induced gas flotation systems** and transfer pumps. Additional facilities include fuel gas treatment, chemical injection systems, oil recovery systems, flare connections, firewater and deluge systems, control and safety systems, substations, and associated civil, structural, mechanical, electrical and instrumentation works.

At **GC-30**, the project includes the development of a treated-water filtration and high-pressure injection system. The scope includes nutshell filters and associated feed pumps with a total capacity of approximately **500,000 barrels of water per day**, together with booster and injection pumps for transferring treated effluent water to designated injection wells.

The GC-30 works also include fuel gas treatment, sludge collection and disposal facilities, oil recovery systems, control and safety systems, substations, laboratory and workshop facilities, and associated civil, structural, piping, mechanical, electrical and instrumentation works.

The project additionally covers **transfer pipelines between GC-25, GC-15 and GC-30**, modifications to existing tanks at GC-30, process and utility tie-ins, electrical and instrumentation modifications, and related infrastructure required to integrate the new facilities with KOC’s existing assets.

MEIL’s responsibilities encompass the **design, engineering, procurement, construction, testing, pre-commissioning, commissioning, start-up and performance testing** of the facilities. The contract scope also includes operation, maintenance, repair and insurance responsibilities for the designated facilities during the applicable O&M period.

The award strengthens MEIL’s presence in Kuwait’s upstream oil and gas sector and adds another major project to its portfolio supporting the development and modernization of KOC’s gathering, separation, water-treatment and injection infrastructure.
