Cosmo Developments Launches AED 6 Billion THE WADI Mixed-Use Project on Yas Island
==================================================================================

[September 19, 2026](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/2026/09/)
[Mohammad S](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/author/ksa-projects/)
[Construction](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/category/construction/)

![](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/wadi-678x381.jpg "wadi")

**Cosmo Developments**, a joint venture between Abu Dhabi-based **Flag Holding Group** and **Reportage Group**, has launched **THE WADI**, a large-scale mixed-use residential development on **Yas Island, Abu Dhabi**, with an estimated investment of **AED 6 billion**.

Fully managed and powered by Reportage Group, THE WADI is being developed on a plot spanning approximately **140,700 square metres** and will comprise around **3,950 residential units**, alongside commercial, recreational and sports facilities and a planned hotel.

The development will offer a mix of **studios and one-, two- and three-bedroom apartments**, creating an integrated residential community supported by a range of lifestyle and leisure amenities.

Advertisement

Located along **Yas Canal on Yas Island**, THE WADI is being positioned as a nature-led waterfront destination inspired by the UAE’s natural landscapes and the movement of water through its valleys. The development’s masterplan integrates residential, commercial, recreational and sports components around a nature-focused community concept.

The project forms part of the ongoing development of Yas Island as a major residential, tourism and leisure destination in Abu Dhabi. THE WADI is approximately **five minutes from Zayed International Airport, nine minutes from Ferrari World Yas Island and 11 minutes from Yas Island’s major attractions**, while central Abu Dhabi is approximately 23 minutes away.

### Project Scope

The AED 6 billion development will include:

* Approximately **3,950 residential units**
* Studios and **one-, two- and three-bedroom apartments**
* Commercial facilities
* Recreational and leisure facilities
* Sports facilities
* A **hotel planned within the masterplan**
* Landscaped and nature-focused community spaces
* Waterfront development along Yas Canal

**Andrea Nucera, Managing Director of Cosmo Developments and Group Managing Director of Reportage Group**, said THE WADI reflects the evolution of Reportage’s approach to developing mixed-use residential communities that respond to their location and the changing needs of residents and the market.

He added that the project introduces a nature-led design philosophy aimed at integrating natural elements into the design and experience of the community, while contributing to Abu Dhabi’s growing appeal as a destination for residents, investors and visitors.

**Adnan Tareen, Managing Director of Flag Holding**, said Yas Island provides a strategic setting for a development of THE WADI’s scale, adding that the project is designed to complement Abu Dhabi’s continued growth as a global destination for living, investment and tourism.

THE WADI is expected to contribute to the expansion of Yas Island’s residential offering by combining **housing, commercial uses, leisure, sports and hospitality facilities** within an integrated waterfront development.
