Hapag-Lloyd adds fourth US$1,000 GRI on ISC-North America trade since May
=========================================================================

[India](/geography/india)

[Pakistan](/geography/pakistan)

[United States](/geography/united-states)

Saturday, September 19, 2026 at 8:12 PM

Share[𝕏](https://twitter.com/intent/tweet?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcontainer-mag.com%2Fupdate%2F20260919-hapag-lloyd-adds-fourth-us-1-000-gri-on-isc-north-america-trade-since-may&text=Hapag-Lloyd%20adds%20fourth%20US%241%2C000%20GRI%20on%20ISC-North%20America%20trade%20since%20May)

Hapag-Lloyd will apply a General Rate Increase and General Rate Adjustment of US$1,000 per container on shipments from the Indian subcontinent, Pakistan and the Middle East to the US East Coast and Houston. The increase covers dry, reefer and special containers in 20-foot and 40-foot sizes, including high-cube equipment. It applies to all containers gated in full from 15 October 2026, valid until further notice. The geographical scope covers India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, the UAE, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman, Kuwait, Iraq, Saudi Arabia and Jordan. It is the fourth US$1,000 increase Hapag-Lloyd has announced on the lane since May.
