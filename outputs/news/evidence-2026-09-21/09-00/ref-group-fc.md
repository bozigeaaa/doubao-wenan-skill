# Group Five, Aramco sign SAR 270.6M contract

20/09/2026 Argaam- Nomu

![](https://argaamplus.s3.amazonaws.com/f0f873d6-a486-4c36-8b25-f19150405a8d.png)

Group Five expects the financial impact in Q2, Q3 2027

* * *

[Group Five Pipe Saudi Co.](http://www.argaam.com/en/tadawul/nomu/group-five) signed a SAR 270.58 million contract with [Saudi Aramco](http://www.argaam.com/en/tadawul/tasi/saudi-aramco), the company said in a statement on **Tadawul**.

The four-month contract covers the manufacture and supply of spiral-welded steel pipes for one of Saudi Aramco’s projects.

The financial impact is expected to be reflected in the second and third quarters of 2027.
