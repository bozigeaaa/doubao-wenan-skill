# East Pipes signs SAR 771M contract with Saudi Aramco

20/09/2026 Argaam

![](https://argaamplus.s3.amazonaws.com/ff97cfbe-2152-4950-ba55-8200f33ea191.png)

East Pipes said the contract covers the manufacturing and supply of steel pipes

* * *

[East Pipes Integrated Company for Industry](https://www.argaam.com/en/tadawul/tasi/east-pipes) signed a contract with Saudi Arabian Oil Co. ( [Saudi Aramco](https://www.argaam.com/en/tadawul/tasi/saudi-aramco)) worth more than SAR 771 million, including VAT.

In a statement on **Tadawul**, the company said the contract has a six-month term and covers manufacturing and supplying steel pipes.

The financial impact of the contract will be reflected from Q4 2026/2027 through Q1 2027/2028.

According to **Argaam**data, this is East Pipes’ second contract with Aramco in September. The company recently signed another contract with the oil giant worth approximately SAR 20 million.
