East Pipes Signs $205 Million Steel Pipe Supply Contract with Aramco
====================================================================

[September 20, 2026](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/2026/09/)
[Mohammad S](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/author/ksa-projects/)
[Oil and Gas](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/category/oil-and-gas/)

**East Pipes Integrated Company for Industry, Saudi Arabia** has signed a contract with **Saudi Arabian Oil Company ([Aramco](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/tag/saudi-aramco/))** for the manufacturing and supply of steel pipes, with a total contract value exceeding **SAR 771 million, including VAT**.

The contract was signed on **September 20, 2026**, and covers the **manufacturing and supply of steel pipes to Aramco**.

Under the contract, East Pipes will manufacture and supply steel pipes in accordance with Aramco’s requirements. The contract has a **six-month duration**.

* **Contractor/Supplier:** East Pipes Integrated Company for Industry
* **Contract Value:** More than **SAR 771 million**, including VAT
* **Scope:** Manufacturing and supply of steel pipes
* **Contract Duration:** Six months
* **Contract Signing Date:** September 20, 2026
