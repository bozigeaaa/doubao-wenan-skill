# Building permits in Saudi Arabia hit 5,828 in July

20/09/2026 Argaam

![](https://argaamplus.s3.amazonaws.com/54ae6596-d0b7-4dfd-9a3d-767eb23306eb.png)

* * *

The number of building permits issued in Saudi Arabia rose 4% year-on-year (YoY) to 5,828 in July 2026, from 5,582 a year earlier.

On a monthly basis, the number of permits increased 16% from 5,045 in June, according to data from the General Authority for Statistics (GASTAT).
