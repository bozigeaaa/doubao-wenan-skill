Riyadh, September 20, 2026, SPA -- Darin and Tarout Island is preparing for renewed economic activity driven by tourism, heritage, and hospitality projects, as well as investments linked to its distinctive natural environment.

Darin and Tarout Island has held historical significance for centuries, with ships once arriving at Darin Port laden with musk, perfumes, textiles, and spices, turning the island into a maritime port and a bustling market for trade in the Arabian Gulf.

The island's history spans more than 5,000 years, and it is home to more than 11 historical and heritage sites. Its significance lies in its ability to combine historical, environmental, and economic value.

A memorandum of understanding between the National Center for Environmental Compliance and the Darin and Tarout Island Development Foundation forms part of a broader project to rehabilitate the island and transform its natural and historical resources into sustainable economic attractions.

The memorandum paves the way for establishing an integrated database of environmental information and studies covering air, water, and soil quality, as well as noise levels. The agreement also provides for identifying priority sites for monitoring and remediation and developing environmental monitoring programs and stations.

It also includes establishing a mechanism for the environmental classification of activities and projects, assessing their environmental performance, and regulating inspection procedures and the reporting of incidents and violations.

The cooperation will also extend to implementing training and awareness programs, supporting initiatives aimed at reducing pollution, and coordinating preparedness for environmental emergencies. A joint committee will be formed to follow up on projects and translate the results of monitoring activities and studies into actionable plans.

Darin and Tarout Island stands as a gateway to investment opportunities, drawing on its ancient history, unique heritage, and natural environment to attract visitors and investors alike.
