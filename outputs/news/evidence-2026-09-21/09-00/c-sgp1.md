Group Five Pipe Saudi Secures $72 Million Saudi Aramco Contract
===============================================================

[September 20, 2026](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/2026/09/)
[Mohammad S](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/author/ksa-projects/)
[Oil and Gas](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/category/oil-and-gas/)

![](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/pipeline-678x381.png "pipeline")

Group Five Pipe Saudi Co. has signed a **SAR 270.58 million (including VAT)** contract with [**Saudi Aramco**](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/tag/saudi-aramco/) for the manufacture and supply of spiral-welded steel pipes for one of the oil giant’s projects in Saudi Arabia.

The contract was signed on **September 17, 2026**, following the award announcement on the same date.

Under the contract, Group Five Pipe Saudi will be responsible for the **manufacturing and supply of spiral-welded steel pipes** specifically tailored to meet the requirements of a Saudi Aramco-owned project.

Advertisement

The contract has a **four-month duration**.

### Project Snapshot

* **Client:** Saudi Aramco
* **Contractor/Supplier:** Group Five Pipe Saudi Co.
* **Contract Value:** SAR 270.58 million, including VAT
* **Scope:** Manufacturing and supply of spiral-welded steel pipes
* **Contract Duration:** 4 months
* **Contract Signing:** September 17, 2026
* **Project Location:** Saudi Arabia
