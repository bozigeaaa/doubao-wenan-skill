OSUS has started construction on OSUS Eye, advancing its planned mixed-use development built around a “10-Minute City” concept that aims to place residential, hospitality, retail, workplace and entertainment uses within a closely connected urban destination.

Construction is progressing under the project’s approved programme, with design, project management and delivery teams coordinating works across the site. OSUS said it is monitoring construction progress, execution quality and the readiness of successive phases, with safety and technical standards forming part of the delivery programme.

The development brings together several international companies across its design and hospitality components. Hilton will operate the hotel and branded residences, while RMJM is responsible for the master design. Cracknell is handling landscape design, with TRI serving as hospitality and entertainment consultant.

OSUS Eye’s “10-Minute City” concept is designed around bringing everyday destinations and services within a more accessible and interconnected urban environment. The developer said the approach is intended to integrate living, working, hospitality, shopping and entertainment while supporting Saudi Arabia’s wider urban development and quality-of-life objectives under Vision 2030.
