NEOM is seeking designers for a freight railway stretching more than 400 kilometres from its Red Sea industrial hub at Oxagon to Saudi Arabia’s national rail network, opening a potential new route for cargo between the kingdom’s two coasts.

The proposed line would connect the Port of NEOM with Al-Jawf province, with the scope covering feasibility studies, concept design and route alignment, according to MEED.

NEOM issued the request on 14 September, with submissions due on 16 September. No construction cost or delivery timeline has been disclosed.

Read more: [Saudi Arabia Railways Receives Bids To Expand Phosphate Rail](https://www.constructionweeksaudi.com/projects-tenders/saudi-bids-phosphate-rail)

Oxagon currently has no rail connection to the kingdom’s national network, meaning cargo moving inland from the port relies on road transport. The move would plug the industrial hub into the 2,750-kilometre North-South Railway, providing a direct freight route toward Saudi Arabia’s mining regions, Riyadh and industrial centres on the Gulf coast.

The link could also give the North-South Railway a new gateway to the Red Sea, creating another route for minerals and other freight moving from northern Saudi Arabia to export markets.

The move comes as NEOM directs further infrastructure investment toward Oxagon. Contractors are preparing bids for a [new wastewater treatment plant,](https://www.constructionweeksaudi.com/gigaprojects/neom-oxagon-wastewater-treatment-plant-tender) while HUMAIN and DataVolt [are developing a planned 1.5-gigawatt](https://www.constructionweeksaudi.com/gigaprojects/neom/neom-360mw-ai-data-center-oxagon) artificial intelligence data centre campus at the industrial hub.
