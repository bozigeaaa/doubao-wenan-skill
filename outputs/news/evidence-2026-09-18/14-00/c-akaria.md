Public Investment Fund-backed Saudi Real Estate Company is seeking contractors for the next phase of its Porta Jeddah development, opening further construction opportunities after awarding the project’s first major package earlier this year.

The developer, known as Al Akaria, has set October 4 as the deadline for contractors to submit prequalification applications for the remaining packages. The scope and value of the upcoming contracts have not been disclosed.

The new procurement round follows Al Akaria’s [463 million riyal ($123 million)](https://www.constructionweeksaudi.com/projects/pif-backed-awards-123-million-contract) award to Mounes Mohamed Alshayeb for Civil Construction, known as MOBCO, in May. That package covers structural works across the development, façades, landscaping and electromechanical works excluding the hotel.

Porta Jeddah is Al Akaria’s first development in the Red Sea city and is planned as a mixed-use destination combining a hotel and serviced apartments with offices, retail, restaurants, cafes, entertainment facilities and public spaces.

Jeddah is drawing a growing pipeline of large-scale real estate investment as developers target demand across residential, hospitality and commercial projects. For Al Akaria, Porta Jeddah marks a geographic expansion beyond its traditional Riyadh base and gives the PIF-backed developer a foothold in one of the kingdom’s largest property markets.
