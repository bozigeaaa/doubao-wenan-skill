Major international contractors are bidding for the Saudi Royal Aviation Village, a $2.5 billion specialized aviation complex that is part of the masterplan for King Salman International Airport in Riyadh. The project is therefore a key infrastructure element for the airport’s operational scope and aviation capabilities. Design-build and construction strategies are being considered by contractors. In the meantime, the contract is expected to be awarded in fall 2026 and construction is expected to begin in 2027.

The Saudi Royal Aviation Village will host the operations of the Saudi royal fleet, executive aviation and specialized support services within the airport. In particular, the complex consists of hangar facilities, maintenance facilities and administrative facilities for high-level aviation activities. The project is thus directly contributing to the airport’s role as a leading regional aviation hub.

Major transport projects are making headway across the Middle East with projects such as the [Hafeet rail project](https://constructionreviewonline.com/3bn-hafeet-rail-project-by-etihad-rail-targets-trial-run-in-q4-2027-connecting-oman-and-uae/) making headway. Hafeet Rail, a joint venture between [Etihad Rail](https://www.etihadrail.ae/), Oman Rail, and Mubadala Investment Company, will begin trial operations in the final quarter of 2027. As a result, the $3 billion railway system is progressing at a healthy pace in both Oman and the UAE.

## King Salman International Airport (KSA) has expanded its specialized aviation capacity.

The $2.5 billion [Royal Aviation Village](https://www.icpcredit.com/Report/ReportRequest?companyID=92177207) enhances the capacity of the airport to handle royal and executive aviation flights in addition to commercial flights. In addition, the airport’s specialized facilities and maintenance capabilities enable it to accommodate international operators and contribute to the modernization of Saudi Arabia’s aviation infrastructure. In particular, the complex is designed to be integrated into the overall master plan of the airport, which is designed to accommodate both civilian and specialized flight operations. The Saudi Royal Aviation Village contract is being bid for by several international construction companies, highlighting the significance of the project in the airport’s development.

## Project Overview

- Project Name: Saudi Royal Aviation Village (King Salman International Airport component)
- Project Value: $2.5 Billion
- Location: King Salman International Airport, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia
- Client: Saudi Aviation Authority
- Status: Contractor bids under preparation, selection fall 2026

Scope

- Royal and executive aviation facility
- Hangar infrastructure
- Maintenance and operational facilities
- Administrative and support services
- Specialized aviation capabilities

Project Highlights

- $2.5 billion specialized aviation complex
- Royal fleet operations support
- Executive aviation services
- King Salman International Airport integration
- Modern facility design

Key Developments

- Contractor bids prepared September 2026
- International firms competing
- Contract award expected fall 2026
- Construction begins 2027

Key Challenges

- Coordinating with larger airport development
- Meeting royal aviation standards
- Managing specialized facility requirements
- Airport masterplan integration

Outlook

- Contractor selection fall 2026
- Construction begins 2027
- Operational launch post-completion
- Supports airport aviation hub positioning
