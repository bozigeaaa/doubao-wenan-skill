            Company Profile

            Company overview

            The company works in the field of developing modern urban destinations with an integrated investment system.

            Company History

            The Company was established in 2012 pursuant to the high order to undertake the development of King Abdulaziz Road Project in Makkah under the name of -Masar Destination- and the Company later expanded its footprint to other destinations.
