Emaar EC Signs SAR 152 Million Infrastructure Development Agreement for Masarat Mobility Park in King Abdullah Economic City
============================================================================================================================

[September 15, 2026](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/2026/09/)
[Mohammad S](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/author/ksa-projects/)
[Construction](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/category/construction/)

![](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/688599115_1447641477392924_3676526229967208723_n-678x381.jpg "688599115_1447641477392924_3676526229967208723_n")

Emaar, The Economic City (EEC) has signed an **Infrastructure Development Agreement valued at SAR 152 million, excluding VAT**, with **Automotive Solutions Company for Logistics Services (Masarat Mobility Park)** to develop additional infrastructure and utility capacity for the Masarat Mobility Park project within the **King Salman Automotive Cluster** at King Abdullah Economic City (KAEC).

The agreement, signed on **13 September 2026**, covers the **design, construction and delivery of additional infrastructure works** required to provide utility services and capacity for the Masarat Mobility Park development in the Special Economic Zone at King Abdullah Economic City.

The additional infrastructure works are being undertaken following a request from Masarat Mobility Park for **utility capacities exceeding those originally contemplated** when the industrial land sale agreements were entered into in 2023.

Advertisement

Under the agreement, Emaar, The Economic City will be responsible for designing, constructing and delivering the required additional infrastructure to support the project’s utility requirements. The works are intended to facilitate the development and operation of Masarat Mobility Park as an integrated industrial and logistics hub serving the Kingdom’s automotive and mobility sector.

Masarat Mobility Park was established by the parties that previously entered into industrial land sale agreements with Emaar EC, including **Dar Al Himmah Projects Co. Ltd., TASARU Mobility Investments, Zamil Group Real Estate Company, and Abdullah Ibrahim Alkhorayef Sons Co.** The venture is being developed to establish secondary infrastructure and an industrial logistics hub within the Special Economic Zones.

The latest infrastructure agreement represents an additional investment associated with the development of the automotive and mobility cluster at KAEC and strengthens the infrastructure base required to support future industrial and logistics activities within the zone.

The financial impact of the agreement is expected to be reflected in **Emaar EC’s financial results from the third quarter of 2026**.

### Project Details

* **Project:** Masarat Mobility Park – Additional Infrastructure Works
* **Location:** King Salman Automotive Cluster, Special Economic Zone, King Abdullah Economic City, Saudi Arabia
* **Developer/Contractor:** Emaar, The Economic City (EEC)
* **Client:** Automotive Solutions Company for Logistics Services (Masarat Mobility Park)
* **Contract Value:** **SAR 152 million**, excluding VAT
* **Agreement Date:** 13 September 2026
* **Scope of Work:** Design, construction and delivery of additional infrastructure and utility works
* **Purpose:** Provision of additional utility capacities required for the Masarat Mobility Park development
* **Financial Impact:** Expected from Q3 2026

### Background

Emaar, The Economic City had previously announced industrial land sale agreements in June 2023 involving several companies for the development of automotive and mobility-related facilities within King Abdullah Economic City. The parties subsequently established **Masarat Mobility Park** to develop secondary infrastructure and create an industrial logistics hub within the Special Economic Zone.

The latest agreement is separate from the previously announced industrial land sale transaction, which had a value of **SAR 366.77 million**, and relates specifically to the additional infrastructure and utility requirements of the Masarat Mobility Park development.
