### Company Profile

**Company overview**

The company activity is acquisition and development of lands and establishment of residential and commercial buildings and selling, leasing, property management for the company and others, purchasing, producing, selling and leasing of the materials and equipment for the construction, and construction for buildings and doing all the work related to the achievement of its objectives and purchasing, selling and renting villas and Duplexes and buildings .The company has developed since it was established some projects wholly owned, including housing projects, commercial and administrative, such as commercial centers ( Olaya I, II and III and the Plaza and Siteen commercials centers in Malaz) and several residential units in Olaya, as well as in Diplomatic quarter (Villas, Duplex , flats).The company also implemented some projects for other parties such as Saudi Embassies in the Gulf Cooperation Council, Deira markets, Al Yamamah Press Establishment and Almuaiglia Markets, the company is currently preparing some designs to execute new projects in Riyadh

**Company History**

Saudi Real Estate Company was established by Royal Decree No. (M / 6) dated 22/03/1385, and the decision of the Council of Ministers No. (1259) dated 16/07 / 1396H, headquartered in the capital.
