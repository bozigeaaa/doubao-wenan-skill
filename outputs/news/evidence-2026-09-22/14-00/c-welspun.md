India’s Welspun Corp Associate Wins $205 Million Aramco Contract
================================================================

[September 21, 2026](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/2026/09/)
[Mohammad S](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/author/ksa-projects/)
[Oil and Gas](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/category/oil-and-gas/)

![](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Welspun-678x381.png "Welspun")

East Pipes Integrated Company for Industry (EPIC), a Saudi Arabia-based steel pipe manufacturer and **associate company of Welspun Corp Limited**, has signed a contract with **Saudi Arabian Oil Company (Aramco)** for the manufacturing and supply of steel pipes valued at **more than SAR 771 million**, including value-added tax.

EPIC, a listed company on the Saudi Exchange, announced the contract award following the signing of the agreement with Aramco. The contract has a **six-month execution period**, with its financial impact expected to be reflected from the fourth quarter of FY2026-27 through the first quarter of FY2027-28.

Under the contract, **EPIC will manufacture and supply steel pipes to Aramco**. The award further strengthens the company’s position as a key domestic supplier supporting Saudi Arabia’s oil and gas sector and strategic energy infrastructure.

Advertisement

EPIC specializes in the production of **Helical Submerged Arc Welded (HSAW) pipes** and operates fully integrated manufacturing facilities in Saudi Arabia.

The company has an established track record in executing large-scale pipe orders while meeting customers’ delivery and quality requirements. The latest contract also supports the Kingdom’s localization and industrial development objectives under **Saudi Vision 2030**.

EPIC is an **associate company of Welspun Corp Limited**, one of the major global manufacturers of large-diameter line pipes. Through its association with Welspun Corp, EPIC benefits from the group’s experience and capabilities in the global pipe manufacturing sector while maintaining its position as a Saudi-based manufacturer serving the Kingdom’s energy and infrastructure market.
