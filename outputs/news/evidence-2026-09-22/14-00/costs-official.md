## CCI in Saudi Arabia rises by 2.3% in <strong>August</strong> 2026

The Construction Cost Index (CCI) recorded a 2.3% increase in August 2026 compared to the corresponding month of the previous year 
(August 2025). This increase is mainly attributed to a 2.1% rise in construction costs for the residential sector and a 2.6% rise in 
construction costs for the non-residential sector (Figure 1).

### Annual change in CCI for the residential sector

CCI for the residential sector increased by 2.1% year-on-year. This 
increase was mainly attributed to the increase in the costs of basic 
materials by 1.8%, driven by an increase in the prices of metal products 
by 2.0%. The costs of renting equipment and machinery also increased by 
4.1%, driven by the increase in the costs of renting equipment and 
machinery with an operator by 5.6%. Labor costs also increased by 5.1%, 
while the energy prices rose by 3.0% (Figure 2).

### Annual change in CCI for the non-residential sector

CCI for the non-residential sector increased by 2.6% year-on-year. This 
increase was attributed to the increase in the costs of renting equipment 
and machinery by 5.8%, driven by an increase of 7.2% in the costs of 
renting equipment and machinery with an operator, a 2.0% increase in 
labor costs, and a 3.0% increase in energy costs. Basic materials costs also 
increased by 1.8%, due to the rise in the prices of other construction
materials by 4.3% and timber and joinery by 2.1% (Figure 3).

#### Methodology and quality

The Construction Cost Index (CCI) reflects price movements in construction input 
costs, consisting of 51 items. The prices of which are collected monthly from 13 
regions through field visits to contractors' offices, engineering offices, and 
establishments selling construction materials. The year 2023 has been set as the 
base year, and the CCI statistics are published monthly. For more details, please
