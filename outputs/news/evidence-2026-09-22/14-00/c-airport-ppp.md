Saudi Arabia Qualifies Six Bidders for Prince Naif Bin Abdulaziz International Airport PPP Project
==================================================================================================

[September 6, 2026](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/2026/09/)
[Mohammad S](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/author/ksa-projects/)
[Construction](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/category/construction/), [PPP](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/category/ppp/)

![](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/airport-terminal-678x381.jpg "airport terminal")

**MATARAT Holding**, in collaboration with the **National Center for Privatization & PPP (NCP)**, has announced the qualification of **six consortia and companies** for the development of the **Prince Naif Bin Abdulaziz International Airport PPP Project** in Qassim, Saudi Arabia.

The qualification represents a key milestone in the Kingdom’s plans to develop and modernize the airport through a **public-private partnership (PPP)** model. The project is planned under a **30-year contract period, including the construction phase**, and is aimed at enhancing the airport’s infrastructure, operational efficiency and passenger services.

The six qualified bidders comprise leading Saudi and international airport operators, infrastructure developers, investors and engineering companies.

Advertisement

### Six Qualified Consortia

**1. Egis – YDA – Safari – Lamar Holding Consortium**

The first consortium comprises **Egis**, **YDA**, **Safari** and **Lamar Holding**, bringing together international engineering and airport development capabilities with Saudi investment and infrastructure expertise.

**2. PPMDC – Al Gihaz Holding Consortium**

The second consortium consists of **Ports Projects Management & Development Company (PPMDC)** and **Al Gihaz Holding**, combining project development, engineering and infrastructure capabilities.

**3. Tanama – Oman Airports – Namaya – Al Bawani Capital Consortium**

The third consortium brings together **Tanama**, **Oman Airports**, **Namaya** and **Al Bawani Capital**, combining airport operating experience with investment and infrastructure development capabilities.

**4. GMR Airports**

**GMR Airports** has qualified as a standalone bidder for the Prince Naif Bin Abdulaziz International Airport project.

**5. Vision Invest – ASYAD – daa International Consortium**

The fifth consortium comprises **Vision Invest**, **ASYAD** and **daa International**, combining Saudi investment capabilities with regional logistics expertise and international airport management experience.

**6. TAV Airports – MADA International Holding Consortium**

The sixth consortium consists of **TAV Airports, a member of Groupe ADP**, and **MADA International Holding**.

### Project Scope

The Prince Naif Bin Abdulaziz International Airport project is being developed as a long-term PPP covering the **design, financing, construction, transfer, operation and maintenance** of the airport. The project site is located approximately **25 kilometres from Buraidah** in the Qassim region.

The proposed development includes a **fully equipped passenger terminal**, supporting airport facilities and the development and upgrade of key airside infrastructure, including the **runway, taxiways and aircraft aprons**.

The project is intended to increase airport capacity and improve passenger experience and operational efficiency, while providing infrastructure capable of meeting future aviation demand in the Qassim region.

The development of Prince Naif International Airport is also expected to strengthen regional air connectivity and support the growth of tourism, business and economic activity in Qassim.

### Supporting Saudi Aviation and Logistics Strategy

The airport development forms part of Saudi Arabia’s broader programme to attract private-sector investment into the aviation sector and develop the Kingdom’s airports under PPP structures.

The project supports the objectives of the **National Transport and Logistics Strategy and Saudi Vision 2030**, which seek to enhance the Kingdom’s transport infrastructure, improve connectivity and establish Saudi Arabia as a leading global logistics and aviation hub.

MATARAT Holding and NCP launched the Expression of Interest process for the project in February 2026, inviting local, regional and international private-sector participants to participate in the airport development.

The qualification of the six groups now marks an important step toward the next stage of the procurement process, bringing the project closer to the selection of a private-sector partner for the long-term development and operation of Prince Naif Bin Abdulaziz International Airport.
