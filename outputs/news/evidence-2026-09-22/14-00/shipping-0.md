# Shipping to Tema, Ghana? A Congestion Destination Surcharge (CGD) is coming up

AfricaGhana

A **Congestion Destination Surcharge (CGD)** from Africa

Mauritania, Senegal, Gambia, Guinee, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Ivory Coast, Ghana, Togo, Benin, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Equatorial Guinea, Congo Brazzaville, DR Congo, Angola, Namibia, South Africa, Kenya, Tanzania, Djibouti and Sudan

, **Asia**

**Brunei, Cambodia, China, Hong Kong (PRC), Taiwan (PRC), Macau (PRC), Indonesia, Japan, Korea, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam**

**, Oceania**

**Australia, Cook Islands, Fiji, Micronesia, New Caledonia, New Zealand, French Polynesia, Papua New Guinea, Solomon Islands, Tonga, Vanuatu, Wallis and Futuna Island**

**, the Middle East**

**UAE, Bahrain, Oman, Iraq, Kuwait, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Jordan**

**and Indian Subcontinent**

**India, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka**

**to Tema, Ghana** is coming up. This surcharge is applicable to **reefer containers** for all sailings commencing on **October 5, 2026**.

The details of this surcharge are listed below:

- **USD 250 per TEU for reefer containers**
