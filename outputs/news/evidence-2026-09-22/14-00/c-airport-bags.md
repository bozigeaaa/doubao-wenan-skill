Leonardo, MAFA and BELTCON Win Madinah Airport Terminal 2 Baggage Handling Contract
===================================================================================

[September 22, 2026](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/2026/09/)
[Mohammad S](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/author/ksa-projects/)
[Transport](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/category/transport/)

![](https://www.saudigulfprojects.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Leonardo1-678x381.jpg "Leonardo1")

Leonardo, MAFA Co. Ltd. and BELTCON have secured a contract to deliver the **automated baggage handling system package for the Terminal 2 expansion at Prince Mohammed bin Abdulaziz International Airport in Madinah, Saudi Arabia**.

The contract covers the **engineering, system integration, manufacturing, installation, testing, commissioning and delivery of the complete baggage handling system**, supporting the expansion of one of the Kingdom’s key international aviation gateways.

The project is operated by **Tibah Airports Operation Company** and marks the **first deployment of Leonardo’s MBHS® Cross-belt Sorter technology in Saudi Arabia**.

Advertisement

### Project Scope

Under the contract, **Leonardo** will supply its MBHS® Cross-belt Sorter together with its **Sort Allocation Computer (SAC)**. The technology will provide automated and intelligent baggage routing for departure, transfer and arrival operations and will be integrated with the airport’s operational systems.

**MAFA Co. Ltd. and BELTCON** will undertake the engineering, system integration, manufacturing, installation, testing, commissioning and delivery of the complete baggage handling system.

The solution is designed to provide high availability, operational flexibility and future scalability as passenger traffic at the airport continues to grow.

Leonardo’s MBHS® Cross-belt Sorter technology has been deployed in more than 50 airport projects worldwide. The system is designed to handle both conventional and irregularly shaped baggage, using linear motor technology, inductive power transmission and wireless communication to provide high-speed and accurate baggage sorting.

### Supporting Madinah Airport Expansion

Prince Mohammed bin Abdulaziz International Airport currently serves approximately **8 million passengers annually**, with the Terminal 2 expansion expected to support the airport’s continued growth.

The new baggage handling system will play a key role in improving the airport’s automated baggage processing capabilities as passenger demand increases, driven by tourism, business travel and the large number of pilgrims visiting Madinah for Hajj and Umrah.

The project also supports the development of Saudi Arabia’s aviation infrastructure under **Saudi Vision 2030**, which targets significant growth in international tourism and air connectivity.

### Project Timeline

Site installation activities are scheduled to **begin in January 2027**, with final system commissioning expected in **April 2027**.

The contract also represents Leonardo’s first introduction of its MBHS® Cross-belt Sorter technology into the Saudi market, while strengthening MAFA and BELTCON’s capabilities in airport baggage handling, automation and integrated material handling solutions.

The partnership brings together Leonardo’s international airport automation technology, MAFA’s regional airport infrastructure experience and BELTCON’s German engineering expertise in baggage handling, conveyor systems, manufacturing and system integration.
